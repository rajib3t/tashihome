The Gap We Saw

For an experience meant to feel like "home," finding one felt anything but trustworthy.

Unlike hotels, homestays in North Bengal have largely stayed word-of-mouth — a phone number passed along by a friend, a DM to an Instagram page with no way to verify if the photos are real, or a booking confirmed only after days of back-and-forth, sometimes falling through at the last minute.

At the same time, homestay owners — many of them families who’ve been hosting for years — had no real platform of their own. They relied entirely on repeat guests and referrals, with no way to reach the growing number of travelers actively looking for exactly what they offered.

"That gap is what led to Tashihomes — North Bengal’s first dedicated homestay booking platform, built to bring structure and trust to both sides."

The Evolution

Replacing Word-of-Mouth Chaos with Trust

How Tashihomes transforms how travelers discover authentic homestays and how hosts welcome guests.

The Word-of-Mouth Past

Uncertain, fragmented, and vulnerable

  • ✕ Unverified photos & listings: Relying on obscure social media posts with no guarantee that rooms look as advertised.
  • ✕ Days of back-and-forth: Inconsistent phone calls, lost WhatsApp messages, and unclear pricing details.
  • ✕ Fragile confirmations: Bookings that fall through at the last minute with no cancellation safeguards or deposits.
  • ✕ Hosts left invisible: Generational homestays unable to reach new travelers beyond old repeat guest networks.

The Tashihomes Standard

Verified, authentic, and seamless

  • ✓ 100% In-Person Verified: Every homestay is physically inspected, with genuine unedited photos of rooms and surrounds.
  • ✓ Direct & Transparent: Instant clarity on amenities, food options, local guidance, and honest transparent pricing.
  • ✓ Locked-in Peace of Mind: Secure deposit protection to confirm the room; settle the rest in person with hosts.
  • ✓ Founding Partner Support: Equipping host families with visibility, fair terms, and respect for their home traditions.
