Nature of the Platform (Marketplace, Not Operator)

This section is the legal foundation of the entire document. Getting it right is what limits Tashi Homes' liability for what happens inside someone's home.

2.1 Intermediary / Marketplace Model

Tashi Homes operates strictly as an online marketplace and intermediary, within the meaning of Section 2(1)(w) of the Information Technology Act, 2000 and Rule 2(1)(w) of the Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, 2020. Tashi Homes:

  • • does not own, lease, manage, or operate any Homestay listed on the Platform;
  • • does not act as an agent of the Host for the purpose of the underlying accommodation contract, which is formed directly between Guest and Host;
  • • does not employ, supervise, or control Hosts, their staff, or their household members;
  • • is not a party to, and assumes no liability under, the contract of accommodation formed between Guest and Host at the point of Booking confirmation.

Tashi Homes' role is limited to: listing discovery, search and comparison tools, secure payment collection and disbursal, booking confirmation logistics, customer support coordination, and (where offered) verification checks on Hosts and properties as described in Section 4.

2.2 Why This Distinction Matters for a Homestay Model

Because Homestays are private residences rather than licensed commercial hotels, Tashi Homes' marketplace-not-operator status is especially important to state clearly to Guests — the safety standards, staffing, and facilities of a family home will differ from a hotel, and Guests are booking with that understanding. Section 3.6 (Guest Acknowledgement of Homestay Nature) makes this explicit as part of the Guest contract.

3.3 Account Registration

  • • Users must provide accurate, current, and complete information at registration and keep it updated.
  • • Users are responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of login credentials and for all activity under their account.
  • • Tashi Homes may suspend or terminate accounts that provide false information, are used fraudulently, or violate these Terms.

3.4 Booking Process

  • • A Booking request becomes a confirmed Booking only upon (a) successful payment or advance payment as specified in the Listing, and (b) confirmation issued by the Platform on the Host's behalf or by the Host directly, whichever workflow is enabled for that Listing.
  • • Prices, availability, and house rules displayed at the time of Booking form part of the contract; Tashi Homes will notify Guests promptly if a Host is unable to honour a confirmed Booking (see Section 3.7 — Host Default).
  • • Guests must review property description, photographs, amenities, and house rules carefully before booking, as Homestays vary significantly in facilities compared to hotels.

3.5 Guest Conduct at the Homestay

Because a Homestay is someone's home, Guest conduct obligations are stricter and more specific than at a hotel:

  • • Guests must comply with the Host's house rules (check-in/out times, guest limits, smoking/alcohol policy, pet policy, quiet hours) as disclosed in the Listing.
  • • Guests must not exceed the disclosed occupancy limit or bring undisclosed guests/pets without the Host's consent.
  • • Guests are liable to the Host directly for any damage to the property, fixtures, or belongings caused during their stay, beyond ordinary wear and tear.
  • • Illegal activity, harassment of the Host or their family, or activity endangering the safety of the household will result in immediate account suspension and may be reported to local authorities.

3.6 Guest Acknowledgement of Homestay Nature

By booking, the Guest expressly acknowledges and accepts that:

  • • a Homestay is a private residence, not a licensed hotel, and may not offer 24-hour reception, elevators, standardised fire-safety infrastructure, or hotel-grade housekeeping;
  • • they may be sharing common areas with the Host's family or other Guests, depending on the Listing type (entire home vs. private room vs. shared homestay);
  • • facilities, décor, and services will vary Host to Host, and the Listing description/photographs are the operative representation of what to expect, not a general hotel-industry standard.

Homestay Partner (Host) Agreement

4.1 Eligibility & Onboarding

  • • The Host must be the legal owner of the property or hold documented authority (e.g., from the owner/family) to let it out for paid stays.
  • • The Host must provide accurate identity documents, property ownership/occupancy proof, and bank/UPI details for payouts, as part of Tashi Homes' onboarding verification.
  • • Tashi Homes may conduct a physical or virtual property verification before activating a Listing, and may periodically re-verify Listings.

4.2 Regulatory & Licensing Compliance (Host's Responsibility)

Homestays in West Bengal — particularly in the hills (Darjeeling, Kalimpong) and Dooars/Terai belt — may be subject to registration under the West Bengal Tourism department's homestay scheme, local municipal/Gram Panchayat trade licence requirements, fire and public-safety norms, and mandatory guest-register/police-verification obligations for tourist lodging. The Host is solely responsible for:

  • • obtaining and maintaining any homestay registration, trade licence, or tourism department certification applicable in their district;
  • • maintaining a guest register and complying with local police reporting/foreigner-registration (FRRO/C-Form) requirements where the Guest is a foreign national;
  • • basic fire and electrical safety measures, and disclosing any known safety limitations of the property;
  • • complying with local municipal, panchayat, and state tourism regulations applicable to paid home-stays.

Tashi Homes may request copies of applicable registrations at onboarding and may de-list any Homestay operating without required local approvals, but Tashi Homes does not certify or guarantee a Host's regulatory compliance, and onboarding a Listing is not a representation that the property fully complies with every applicable local law.

4.3 Listing Accuracy

  • • Hosts must ensure photographs, descriptions, amenities, pricing, and availability calendars are accurate and kept up to date.
  • • Misrepresentation (e.g., undisclosed shared bathrooms, inaccurate location, unlisted extra charges) is grounds for Listing suspension and forfeiture of that Booking's payout, without prejudice to the Guest's refund.

4.4 Commission, Fees & Payouts

  • • Tashi Homes charges a commission/service fee on each completed Booking, as disclosed to the Host at onboarding and reflected in the Host's dashboard (exact percentage to be fixed commercially and inserted here).
  • • Payouts to Hosts will be released to the registered bank/UPI account within the timeline specified in the Host dashboard (e.g., T+2 days after check-in), net of commission, applicable TDS under the Income Tax Act, 1961 where required, and any Guest-approved refund deductions.
  • • Hosts are independently responsible for their own GST/income tax registration and compliance on income earned through the Platform; Tashi Homes may issue TDS certificates as required by law but does not act as the Host's tax advisor.

4.5 Cancellation by Host

Hosts who cancel a confirmed Booking without a valid reason (as opposed to a genuine emergency) may be subject to a cancellation penalty deducted from future payouts, temporary suspension of their calendar, or removal from the Platform for repeat cancellations, in addition to the Guest refund obligations in Section 3.7.

4.6 Non-Circumvention

Hosts and Guests agree not to use contact information exchanged through the Platform to complete a booking transaction outside the Platform in order to avoid the Service Fee, during the term of these Terms and for a reasonable period thereafter. Confirmed circumvention may result in account termination and recovery of the bypassed commission.

4.7 Termination / De-listing

Tashi Homes may suspend or permanently de-list a Homestay for: repeated Guest complaints, safety concerns, regulatory non-compliance, fraudulent listings, discriminatory refusal of Guests, or breach of this Agreement, with notice where practicable except in cases of urgent safety risk.

Cancellation & Refund Policy

Tashi Homes offers tiered cancellation policies that Hosts may choose from per Listing (or per season), disclosed clearly to the Guest before Booking. A suggested default framework is set out below; the actual policy applicable to a specific Booking is the one shown at the time of booking.

6.1 Suggested Standard Tiers

Flexible

  • • Full refund if cancelled 48+ hours before check-in.
  • • 50% refund if cancelled within 48 hours of check-in.
  • • No refund for no-shows or same-day cancellation.

Moderate

  • • Full refund if cancelled 7+ days before check-in.
  • • 50% refund if cancelled 3–7 days before check-in.
  • • No refund within 72 hours of check-in.

Strict / Peak Season

  • • 50% refund if cancelled 14+ days before check-in.
  • • No refund within 14 days of check-in — reflecting the limited inventory and high demand of peak-season North Bengal travel (e.g., Durga Puja, Diwali, and Darjeeling's Oct–Dec/Apr–May peak tourist windows).

6.2 Non-Refundable Components

The Tashi Homes Service Fee is non-refundable in cases of Guest-initiated cancellation, but is refunded in full where the Host cancels, fails to honour a Booking, or where the property is found to be materially misrepresented.

6.3 Refund Timelines

Approved refunds will be processed to the original payment method within 7–10 business days, subject to the Guest's bank/payment provider processing times.

6.4 Force Majeure Cancellations

Where a Booking cannot be honoured due to circumstances beyond either party's reasonable control — landslides, road closures, extreme weather, bandhs/strikes affecting the North Bengal hill routes, government-imposed travel restrictions, or natural disasters — Tashi Homes will facilitate a full refund or free rescheduling, regardless of the standard cancellation tier, unless the Host has already incurred non-recoverable costs which will be disclosed transparently.

6.5 Disputed Quality Issues

If a Guest reports on arrival that the property materially differs from the Listing (cleanliness, safety, or missing disclosed amenities) and this is substantiated (e.g., via photos and Host acknowledgment), Tashi Homes may facilitate a partial or full refund, relocation, or goodwill credit at its discretion, following its internal grievance process (Section 9).

Harassment, threats, or unsafe conduct by either Guest or Host toward the other — including inappropriate behaviour toward Host family members, who often live on the same premises in a genuine homestay — will result in immediate account suspension pending investigation, and may be reported to local police where warranted.
